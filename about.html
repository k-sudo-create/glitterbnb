About Us

Glitter BnB was designed to create a fun and unique stay in Los Angeles. Each room in our BnB has pink glitter walls including three bedrooms, a living room, kitchen and one bathroom. Our property has a outdoor pool with deck chairs and palm trees. We are passionate to make each guest feel comfortable during their stay and to have both a unforgettable and fabulous stay.
