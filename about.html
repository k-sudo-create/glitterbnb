About Us

Glitter BnB was designed to create a fun and unique stay in Los Angeles. Each room in our BnB has pink glitter walls including three bedrooms, a living room, kitchen and one bathroom. Our property has an outdoor pool with deck chairs and palm trees. We are passionate about making each guest feel comfortable during their stay and providing an unforgettable and fabulous experience.
